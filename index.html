Our Story

A Taste of India in the Heart of Vijayawada

At Spice Garden, we bring together the rich flavours of South India, the royal heritage of North Indian cooking, and the bold wok-fired energy of Chinese cuisine — all under one roof.

Every dish is prepared with hand-ground spices, farm-fresh ingredients, and recipes passed down through generations. Whether it's a crispy masala dosa, a slow-cooked biryani, or a sizzling plate of Hakka noodles, our kitchen serves food made with love and served with pride.
